01 · Overview

Project overview

Introduction

BuiltGlory buys and sells homes, and verifies every one first. Its onboarding puts the promise up front: buy verified properties, legal-verified real-estate investment with complete transparency, site-inspected before listing. Buyers find a property, visit it and pay a refundable token; sellers list a property, get it verified and receive an offer.

Behind the app, an admin console runs both sides as pipelines: acquiring homes from sellers, and selling them to buyers.

My role

As UX/UI Executive, I designed the customer app prototype, 96 screens covering buying, selling, the profile and settings, and every system state, and the admin dashboard prototype that the operations team works in.

02 · The challenge

The challenge

A home is the largest purchase most people make, and the paperwork behind it is where trust breaks down. Buyers need to see that a property is real, inspected and legally clean; sellers need to know where their listing stands and what it is worth; and the team in between has to move each deal through many steps without losing any of them.

What made it hard

  • Two journeys in one app

    Buying and selling share one app and one account, but follow completely different flows.

  • Proof, not promises

    Inspection, legal verification and documents have to be visible to the customer, not just done behind the scenes.

  • Long, staged deals

    From enquiry to registration, a deal passes through visits, payments and documentation over weeks.

  • Buyers far away

    Some buyers live abroad (NRIs) and can’t visit in person.

03 · Goals

What it had to do

For the business

  • Run acquisition and sales as clear, staged pipelines
  • Track every legal document per property
  • Catch overdue call-backs and SLA risks early

For the people using it

  • Buyers: see the proof, visit (in person or by video) and pay safely
  • Sellers: list in a few steps and always know the verification status
  • Everyone: know exactly where a deal stands

04 · The solution

The solution

A customer app and an admin console, shown below in screens from their prototypes. Properties, prices and names are sample data.

01. Finding a verified home

Buyers browse verified listings and explore a property in depth before they ever enquire.

What’s in the design

  • Filters plus advanced filters, with verified and ready-to-move tags on every listing
  • Property detail with floor plan, specifications, advantages and an aerial view
  • A 360° virtual tour by room, and compare before you enquire

Why it matters

Showing the verification and the property itself in detail answers the first question a buyer has, “is this real?”, before they spend time on a visit.

02. From enquiry to token payment

Once a buyer enquires, the app walks them through the visit, the documents and a refundable token payment.

What’s in the design

  • Visit booking with a calendar and time slots, plus rescheduling and an NRI video-call option
  • The legal documents shared in the app (sale deed, approvals, layout plan, legal opinion), each verified by the team
  • A refundable token payment held in escrow, with bank transfer, UPI or cheque

Why it matters

Each step shows the buyer what they get and what is protected, the verified documents and the escrow, so the commitment grows with the trust.

03. Selling, with the status always visible

Sellers list a property in a few steps, then follow its verification and receive an offer.

What’s in the design

  • A step-by-step listing: address, type, details, amenities, photos and expected price, with draft saving
  • Verification in six visible steps: submitted, verification call, documents, site inspection, legal check and valuation
  • An offer with its basis explained, a countdown, and accept or negotiate

Why it matters

Selling a home is anxious waiting. A six-step status and an offer that explains itself replace “we’ll call you” with something the seller can check any time.

04. The admin console

Operations runs both sides from one dashboard: enquiries, an acquisition pipeline, a sales pipeline, properties, users and reports.

What’s in the design

  • Two pipelines as stage boards: acquisition (review to seller payout) and sales (leads to closed deal)
  • Enquiries in one place: buy enquiries, sell requests, call-backs, visits, interior leads and chat
  • A legal document checklist per record, grouped into registration, revenue, approval and tax records
  • An overview with enquiries, deals, revenue and SLA alerts

Why it matters

Every screen in the app has a matching step for the team. Mirroring the customer’s journey in the pipelines keeps both sides talking about the same deal in the same words.

05 · Design details

Details that matter

  • Every state designed

    Payment failure, withdrawn and sold-out properties, rejected listings, empty lists, no internet, force update, maintenance and session expiry.

  • Help in context

    Coach marks on first use, FAQs by topic and a call-us option inside settings.

  • Track your own deals

    My Listings, My Visits and My Deals, each with progress you can open.

06 · Next steps

Where it goes next

  1. 01

    Test the token step

    Watch real buyers reach the token payment and see where they hesitate.

  2. 02

    Time each verification stage

    Measure how long listings sit in each of the six stages.

  3. 03

    Bring the extras in

    Favourites, compare, map view and negotiation chat are prototyped; fold them into the main flows.
