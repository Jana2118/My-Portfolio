01 · Overview

Project overview

Introduction

BKKK Casino is a real-money mobile app that puts sports betting and casino games in one place: pre-match and live betting, slots, table games and live dealers, under one account and one balance. Its line on the splash screen says it plainly: Every Game. Every Edge. One App.

It is built for speed, with fast bet placement, real-time odds, instant deposits and same-day withdrawals, and a home feed that surfaces the sports, leagues and games each person actually plays.

My role

As UX/UI Executive, I designed the clickable mobile prototype, working from the product’s screen-by-screen flow documentation and extending it to every state the app can be in: 145 screens across 15 groups. I also designed the operations console the team uses to run the platform.

02 · The challenge

The challenge

People who bet on sport and play casino games usually juggle separate apps, logins and wallets. The app had to bring them together and stay fast, while working inside some of the strictest rules any consumer product faces: identity checks, location checks, age limits, spending limits and self-exclusion.

What made it hard

  • Two products, one app

    A sportsbook and a casino have different rhythms, yet they had to share one account, one balance and one navigation.

  • Speed under pressure

    Live odds change by the second, and markets can be suspended mid-match; the bet slip has to keep up.

  • Regulated at every step

    Real money only after identity verification, and only where wagering is legal, so location is checked before play.

  • Care built in

    Stake limits, cooling-off and self-exclusion have to be clear and firm, without feeling like punishment.

03 · Who it’s for

Four kinds of player

The flow documentation defines who the app serves and what each person can do at each stage of their account. Designing for all of them at once shaped the home feed, the navigation and every gate in the app.

  • Weekend bettor

    The primary user: bets around major sporting events and plays casino games now and then.

  • Daily sports bettor

    Checks odds every day and places many smaller bets across several leagues.

  • Casino player

    Prefers slots and table games, and uses the sportsbook for big events.

  • Cross-product power user

    Plays both heavily and responds to promotions.

04 · Goals

What it had to do

For the business

  • One account and one balance across sportsbook and casino
  • Same-day withdrawals instead of the usual multi-day wait
  • One rewards ladder spanning sports bets and casino play

For the people using it

  • Place a bet in under 60 seconds
  • Track live bets on matches in progress
  • Manage limits and preferences in one place

05 · The solution

The solution

A clickable prototype of the whole app. Teams, odds, balances and games in the screens below are sample data.

01. Trust before the first bet

From the first launch, the app checks who you are and where you are, and explains why.

What’s in the design

  • A KYC hub with three steps (details, ID upload, verified) and why it’s required, in plain words
  • Location access requested before play, with the reason shown up front
  • A clear “not available here” screen that names the detected location and offers where you can play

Why it matters

Regulation decides when someone can play; the design decides whether they understand why. Explaining each check at the moment it happens turns a blocker into a step people can follow.

02. One home for sport and casino

A personalised home feed puts live matches, today’s fixtures and boosts first, and the casino is one tab away, on the same balance.

What’s in the design

  • Sport filters, live matches with scores and odds, and featured fixtures on one feed
  • Casino lobby with featured, slots, table and live-dealer tabs
  • Table games with large, thumb-friendly actions

Why it matters

Each kind of player has a main habit, sport or casino, but most dip into the other. A shared balance and one navigation bar let them cross over without a second app, login or wallet.

03. Betting fast, even live

From a match to a placed bet in a few taps, with live odds and the state of every bet always visible.

What’s in the design

  • Markets grouped as moneyline, spread and totals, with a guided same-game-parlay builder
  • A live tracker with score and stats next to the markets
  • My Bets with leg-by-leg status for every open bet

Why it matters

Speed is the product’s promise, but a fast bet is only good if people know exactly what they backed. Grouped markets and per-leg status keep both.

04. Money in, money out

One wallet for both products, with deposits in a couple of taps and withdrawals tracked to the end.

What’s in the design

  • Balance split into withdrawable and bonus money, so nothing is a surprise at cash-out
  • Quick deposit amounts, saved cards and alternative methods
  • Withdrawal progress shown on the wallet itself

Why it matters

Money is where trust is won or lost. Showing what can be withdrawn, and where a withdrawal is, answers both questions before anyone has to ask.

05. States that protect

Every edge case has its own screen, including the ones that say no.

What’s in the design

  • A stake-limit sheet that states the limit and offers one clear way forward
  • Suspended markets marked in place while prices update
  • Session timeout, maintenance, offline, update-required and account-suspended screens

Why it matters

In a real-money app, the error states are part of responsible design. A firm, calm “not now” protects the player and the operator better than a vague failure.

06. The operator’s side: one console

Behind the app, staff run the platform from an operations console: players, KYC, casino and sportsbook trading, live risk and payments, in one secure command centre.

What’s in the design

  • Staff sign-in with two-factor codes, then a role and permission check before the console opens
  • 16 modules, from users and KYC to sportsbook, live operations, risk, wallet and finance, promotions, support and reports
  • An overview where every card opens its module, and critical risk alerts such as sharp betting and suspicious patterns
  • Queues for KYC reviews and pending withdrawals

Why it matters

The player’s protections only work if the team can act on them. Putting KYC, risk alerts and withdrawals one click from the overview keeps the operator as careful as the app asks players to be.

06 · Design details

Details that matter

  • Every state, not just the happy path

    Wrong, expired and too-many-tries codes; loading, empty and error states; and system screens from geo-block to update required.

  • A navigator for the whole app

    The prototype opens with a screen navigator grouped by flow, so anyone reviewing it can jump straight to any of its 145 screens.

  • Reasons, not just rules

    Location, identity and limit screens each say why they exist, in one plain sentence.
